A little tea inspiration

Notes worth steeping on.

Simple ideas to help you discover a new flavour and get a little more out of your next cup.

The tea basics · 3 min read

A gentle introduction to green tea.

Start simple, explore the flavour, and find the cup you want to come back to.

Read the tea note ↗

Try a small cup of plain green tea first, then notice how its aroma and flavour change as it cools. Experiment with your preferred strength before adding milk or sweetness.

When brewing, follow the temperature and timing on your tea's packaging. For matcha, whisk the powder with a little water before topping up your cup.

Fresh ideas · 2 min read

Give your afternoon a citrus twist.

A few familiar ingredients can make your tea break feel like something new.

Read the tea note ↗

Brew your favourite tea, let it cool, and pour it over ice. Add a slice of orange or lemon and taste before adding any sweetness.

For a more fragrant glass, gently squeeze a little citrus peel above the cup. Adjust the balance to your own taste and enjoy your fresh pour.

Slow living · 3 min read

Make a little room for your tea ritual.

You don't need a perfect morning. Just a few minutes and a cup you enjoy.

Read the tea note ↗

Choose a cup you like, put your phone aside, and take a moment to notice the aroma before your first sip. A ritual can be that simple.

If milk tea is your favourite, brew the tea before adding milk. Taste as you go, adjusting the ratio until the tea and creaminess feel balanced to you.
